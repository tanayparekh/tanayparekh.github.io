Warehouse Scanner — GS1 EPCIS Aggregation 2024
Desktop tool · pharmaceutical serialization
  • Reader link. Opens a TCP/IP connection to a Keyence fixed-mount reader on the plant network and registers an asynchronous callback, so decoded scans arrive on a background thread and get marshalled onto the UI thread as they land. Read cycles are started and stopped remotely through the SDK's LON/LOFF trigger commands, with the reader's live camera feed embedded directly in the form.
  • Scan normalization. Raw reads are cleaned before they reach the work list — GS1 group separators and carriage returns stripped, multi-code payloads split on the delimiter, and anything shorter than a valid 18-character barcode rejected. Duplicate child codes are refused at add time.
  • Aggregation flow. Operators build a pallet by scanning child case codes (GS1 SGTIN), review them in an indexed grid with per-row removal and a live count, then scan the parent pallet code (SSCC) to close the aggregation out.
  • Barcode → EPC. Both formats are parsed into EPC URNs by hand: the SSCC split into extension digit, seven-digit company prefix and serial reference; the SGTIN into GTIN, packing level, item reference and twelve-digit serial.
  • EPCIS generation. Builds the GS1 EPCIS 1.2 document node by node against the urn:epcglobal:epcis:xsd:1 namespace — EPCISDocument → EPCISBody → EventList → AggregationEvent — carrying UTC event time and zone offset, an ADD action, a packing business step, in_progress disposition, and SGLN read point and business location. Files land timestamped as yyyyMMddHHmmss.xml.
  • Delivery. Ships the finished document to the trading partner over SFTP, creating the remote directory if it doesn't already exist. Host, port, credentials, camera IP, and local/remote paths live in an app-managed XML config that self-creates with sane defaults on first run, and SFTP toggles off for local-only operation.
